A Technician

projects a building-attractions
 

A technician from Veliko Tarnovo projects building-attractions. The projects of the 62-years old Peter Djurov are for hotels, cafeterias, complexes fountains, houses. The citizen of Tarnovo is also author of bigger projects for original entertainment bases, sea and mountain resorts, villa zones. One of his most attractive developments is a hotel in a shape of medieval frigate. The unusual building is on 3 foors. The ship’s sail are piece from the original architecture and are raised 4 etages above the building. In they also are located a hotel rooms. The building technician has predicted a sails to be made from uncolored poly carbon, and in a night rime with special projectors to light in the colors of the Bulgarian tricolour. Into the fundament of the hotel there is a flooring which means a sea waves. On the nose of the frigate the projector had put a sailor the puppet will be moved by inplanted mechanic. On the upper deck there is an uncovered swimming pool, and also a gun, which fires salutes for a joking of the guests. In opinion of Mr. Djurov, the hotel will take a place around 1000sq.m. To raise a building effects, it may be raised in a shallow place into the sea, or in a dam lake. “Each one of my architectural ideas has his own philosophy. The buildings, which I’ve projected are with high from 2 to 50 floors”, explained Peter Djurov and shows a whole roll from plans of unique buildings The building technician is projected cafeterias under the shape of a flying teller, a snail, a whali, a pyramid, cigar. “I have a developments for geometrical house, for bio-antipattogenall building, for park-museum of the nations, 3 variants for cascade fountain with attractions, for cafeteria in a mountain area “Bird’s kingdom”. An interest for the building managers are my attraction cafeterias “Zodiac”, “Universe”, “The seven wonders of the world”, “The sunk Atlantida”, counts the resourceful citizen of Turnovo. He is developed also automated system for protection of the buildings from disastrous floods and installations for out flooding of the buildings from a draining and channel waters. That is realized by a dozen of pumps which are activated by electric power or diesel generators. Peter Djurov also offers and the original ideas for greening. “With a Realizing of my projects, the cities will be an attractive places for a relaxation and tourism for Bulgarians and foreigners. “ is categorical the 62-years old citizen of Turnovo.
