Building Chameleon

with changing facade projects a citizen of Tarnovo
 

BUILDING, WHICH COULD MAY CHANGE ITS FACADE PROJECTS THE CITIZEN OF TARNOVO PETER DJUROV. Until now his project wasn’t shown to no one, but he was provoked from publications in the medias, where he wrote, that a florentinian architect has done a rotating skyscraper. “It was written, that he is the first man, who puts the end of the static architecture and starts the beginning of the dynamic. But mine building is from 1999j., which means that Veliko Tarnovo may pretend, that it has a citizen, who is the beginner of a dynamic architecture, smiles Peter Djurov. His building is called “The green collossus”, or “The chameleon”. It will be lighted so different, that every second will change its colors, and because ot that its name isn’t chosen accidently. It’s projected to be with 40-50 floors, richly planted. The building will look alive at the day, and at night, promises the projector. A WOMAN’S FIGURE PRESENTS THE WESTERN FACING, WHICH EXEPTING MADONNA ALSO HAS A FALLING WATERFALL FROM A HIGHT. The women’s eyebrow will move, a veil, which covers her face, will be also removing. A north facing, and also a south are different with their two oriel domed floors with ahanging gardens. The building has an effect roof with a large terrace, on which could land a helicopters. On the top of the roof from metal elements will be made a flower, which could open and close its peatls. From them will be spread a light lights. There will be a little pool, a cafeteria, and a diving elevator. “The elevator will go from the gallery on the first storey, it will cross through the in side of two first storey floors, and after that next up before the facing of the building. Second time it dives across an oriel floors, as it even crosses the middle of an aquarium to make an impression, that those, who are transporting themselves for a piece of a second under the water”, explains his plans and ideas Peter Djurov. As it comes on the surface from the ocean floor, an elevator is rising up over the facing of the building and its third diving is into the panoramian restaurant on the top of the building. But the dynamics of the building comes from a builded with a double walls and attics several rooms. “It will have something like a second room, inserted in the bigger one, something like the Russian matryoshka. They will have a same floor, and hidravlic movement the inner room will prortrude 2-2.5 meters forward over the terrace, and that will rise it’s inside measurements”, says Peter Djurov and believes that it would be a real attraction, if the rooms are lighted in other colours. The building of the citizen of Tarnovo has the other effective inovations. The projector is finished the question with the lightning so, that it could be exchanged on every second. UNLESS THIS ROTATING FACING, PETER DJUROV IS PROUD WITH HIS IDEA FOR A BULGARIA’S SYMBOL CENTER. The center is projected specially for Sofia and is called “Bulgaria – symbol”. However it’s not a building, but a place with remarkable shape. On each side of the place there will be made a building - symbol, in the middle there will be a carpent from grass, on which will be written “The union makes the power”. On this way the citizen of Tarnovo has decided to finis a dispute for Bulgaria’s symbol and to join all significant buildings in the country on one place, which pulls Bulgaria with his energy only forward. More than 60 original ideas for building has projected Peter Djurov. One of them is an idea for a business center in Sofia with the poetical name “Crystal Vitosha”. “I exactly have many projects for Sofia and I decided to offer them to the communal council in the capital. May be some of my ideas to be chosen from the citizens of Sofia”. says Peter Djurov, until open his plans. And suddenly his mind is crossed by an idea for the one of his houses of the future. In the plans of the projector this building could be folded, if its owners wish and to move it. On defined place the house could be hidden under the ground.
